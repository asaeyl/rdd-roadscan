NOTES
  - SYNTHETIC-VIEW PILOT: every positive pair is a lightly altered copy of the same crop, not a second photo of the same real damage. Do not read these numbers as accuracy on real duplicate reports.
  - Nothing is trained and no image is cropped here: the crops, queries and pair labels come ready-made from the dataset zip.
  - The threshold of each method is chosen on the calibration pairs (best F1) and measured on the test pairs; ROC-AUC uses the balanced test pairs.
  - Retrieval (Recall@1, Recall@5, MRR) ranks the true crop against the WHOLE gallery. Road photos from the same drive are often near-identical, so a different crop can look like the right one: this lowers Recall@1 for every method.
